# Flagstaffe — Firm Profile

## Company overview

Flagstaffe is a nationwide FF&E supply and installation specialist for purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA), build-to-rent (BTR), co-living, and multi-room residential schemes across the UK.

The business is jointly owned and managed by **Dan Brownsword** and **Kam Krupinski**, two FF&E professionals who have worked together for over 20 years in senior management positions at two previous market-leading businesses. Between them they have delivered more than **100,000 rooms** — including some of the UK's largest and most award-winning FF&E packages.

## Our services

Flagstaffe provides an end-to-end FF&E service:

1. **Design input** — working with your design team and architect from an early stage to incorporate FF&E into the plan
2. **Turnkey supply** — procurement of bedroom furniture, kitchen units, loose goods, appliances and accessories
3. **Bespoke manufacturing** — custom furniture to any size, shape or finish using Kronospan, Egger, Pfleiderer, Swiss Krono and Finsa materials
4. **Installation** — CSCS-accredited fitters, locally based to each site, scaled to programme
5. **Project co-ordination** — end-to-end FF&E workstream management

### Scope typically includes:

- **Bedroom furniture:** beds, desks, wardrobes, drawers, mirrors, pinboards, coathooks
- **Kitchen units (KLD):** wall units, base units, tall units, worktops, splashbacks
- **Loose goods:** mattresses, sofas, desk chairs, dining furniture, coffee tables, bins, accessories, brown goods
- **Appliances:** ovens, hobs, extractors, microwaves, fridge-freezers (Montpellier standard)
- **Sinks and taps:** Blanco sink and tap (dry fit; wet connections by others)

### Standard exclusions (all projects):

- VAT (reverse charge likely applies)
- Skips (by Main Contractor)
- All electrical connections other than plug-in
- All wet/plumbing connections
- Sink traps
- Scribing to uneven floors or walls
- Sparkle clean (builder's clean included)
- Commissioning of electrical or wet connections

## Delivery terms (standard)

- Quotation valid 60 days
- Pricing based on whole order placed with Flagstaffe — changes to scope may affect pricing
- Models and specification may change during project; Flagstaffe reserves right to propose current alternatives
- Horizontal distribution included; vertical distribution by Main Contractor (goods hoist or forklift)
- Articulated lorry access required; off-load within 25m of vertical distribution point
- Sequencing to be agreed for sufficient rooms available per visit
- Final pricing subject to site survey
- Design responsibility remains with principal designer; sign-off required before bulk manufacturing
